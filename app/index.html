Back up before enabling developer mode.

Files fully synced to the reMarkable cloud will return when you sign in and pair your tablet with the same account. Finish syncing first; back up any unsynced files.

Developer mode & compatibility
  1. Back up your files and verify the backup.
  2. On Paper Pro: Settings → General → Paper Tablet → Software → Advanced → Developer Mode. Follow the on-device reset prompts.
  3. Finish setup and connect tablet Wi-Fi.
  4. Find the SSH password: Settings → General → Help → About → Copyrights and Licenses → General Information.
  5. Connect directly with a USB data cable. Keep the tablet awake during installation.

Supported: Paper Pro (Ferrari), firmware 3.29.0.153 or 3.29.1.0. Other devices and firmware are refused. Do not downgrade for this app.
